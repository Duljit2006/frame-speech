The Code-Switching Challenge

Why standard monolithic transcription engines fail on multilingual Indian speech.

Standard ASR Models (Whisper alone)

Monolithic AI models assume the entire conversation is in one language. When speakers switch languages mid-sentence, the model hallucinates or force-transliterates words phonetically.

// Audio: "In Hindi we say समय, too different!"

❌ Standard Output:
"In Hindi we say samay, too different!" (Forces Latin script)
- OR -
"इन हिंदी वी से समय टू डिफरेंट" (Forced Devanagari)

FrameSpeech Pipeline Solution

FrameSpeech tracks language switches second-by-second with SpeechBrain and Gemini, preserving English loanwords in Latin script while rendering Indic text in its true native alphabet.

// Audio: "In Hindi we say समय, too different!"

✅ FrameSpeech Output:
"In Hindi we say समय, too different!"
(Perfect word-level bilingual code-switching)

7-Stage System Pipeline

How raw audio travels through specialized AI models and post-processing stages.

Stage 01

Audio Extractor

Rips audio from YouTube URLs or local files using yt-dlp and standardizes it to 16kHz Mono 16-bit PCM WAV via FFmpeg.

audio_extractor.py
Stage 02

Silero VAD

Lightweight neural Voice Activity Detector on CPU that strips non-speech silence and music, saving up to 40% GPU processing time.

vad.py
Stage 03

Segmentation

Slices continuous speech into 3.0-second sliding windows with a 1.0-second stride to capture boundary phonetic transitions.

segmentation.py
Stage 04

SpeechBrain LID

Batched ECAPA-TDNN neural classifier (107 languages) with Indian region whitelisting and lazy Whisper fallback.

lid_processor.py
Stage 05

Timeline Smoother

Applies time-bin majority voting and multi-pass glitch absorption (2.5s threshold) to eliminate split-second AI hallucinations.

smoothing.py
Stage 06

faster-whisper

CTranslate2 int8 transcription engine with gated language hinting (>85% confidence), block merging, and the Assamese Hack.

transcriber.py
Stage 07

Gemini AI Corrector

Full-context two-pass LLM restructuring that merges sentence fragments, enforces code-switching, and formats Assamese orthography.

text_corrector.py

Key Technical Innovations

Novel engineering solutions developed to solve complex multimodal and hardware bottlenecks.

The Assamese Script Hack

Whisper lacks native Assamese acoustic models and outputs Romanized gibberish. We route Assamese speech through Whisper's strong Bengali Eastern Nagari model, then use Gemini in Stage 7 to restore authentic Assamese orthography (র → ৰ, ব → ৱ).

GPU VRAM Load-Offload-Swap

Running SpeechBrain ECAPA-TDNN and Whisper large-v3 concurrently exceeds 4GB VRAM. We engineered an automatic offload-swap lifecycle that dynamically moves models between GPU and CPU to fit on consumer RTX 2050 hardware without OOM crashes.

Zero-Risk GFLOPs Optimization

Reduced inference time by 20% on a 10-minute multilingual video by batching LID chunks into 32-tensor GPU forward passes, eliminating redundant double-VAD calls, and merging same-language blocks prior to Whisper execution.

Full-Context Two-Pass Gemini Batching

To avoid output token cutoffs while preserving narrative continuity, the full transcript context is passed as read-only background while segments are processed in batches of 100 with dynamic 429 exponential backoff handling.

Dual Language Summary Analytics

The interactive frontend provides side-by-side comparative distribution charts contrasting raw acoustic audio predictions (SpeechBrain) with semantic post-correction results (Gemini).

Real-time SSE Job Streaming

FastAPI Server-Sent Events (SSE) stream live progress step-by-step to the browser, driving an animated 3-stage progress stepper and live elapsed timer.
